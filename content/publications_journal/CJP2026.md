abstract: "We perform a full three-dimensional particle-in-cell (3D-PIC) simulation to investigate a phase-matching scheme of ion-based high-order harmonic generation (HHG). A high-intensity laser pulse (~10<sup>16</sup> W/cm<sup>2</sup>, 405-nm wavelength, 50-fs pulse duration) is employed to produce He ions as the interacting medium for extending the cutoff photon energy to the water-window X-ray spectral region. The simulation result shows that phase matching for the 179<sup>th</sup> harmonic (2.26 nm) is achieved by compensating the positive dipole phase with negative plasma and geometric phase shifts through laser divergence control. The relative conversion efficiency can be higher than 91% of the value with the ideal phase-matching condition."
